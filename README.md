# Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee Attrition Analysis is a data analytics project focused on analyzing employee turnover and identifying key factors associated with employee attrition.

The project analyzes **1,470 employee records** using **Advanced Excel and SQL** to uncover attrition patterns across departments, job roles, overtime, age groups, job satisfaction, income, and years at the company.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition
- Identify attrition patterns across departments and job roles
- Examine the relationship between overtime and attrition
- Analyze attrition across different age groups
- Study the impact of job satisfaction on attrition

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### Department Analysis
Analyzed employee attrition across different departments to identify variations in turnover levels.

### Job Role Analysis
Compared attrition across different job roles to identify roles with higher employee turnover.

### Overtime Analysis
Analyzed attrition based on overtime status to understand its association with employee turnover.

### Age Group Analysis
Segmented employees into different age groups and compared attrition rates.

### Job Satisfaction Analysis
Examined employee job satisfaction levels in relation to attrition.

### Income Analysis
Analyzed monthly income patterns for employees who stayed and employees who left.

### Tenure Analysis
Analyzed years at company and other tenure-related attributes to understand employee retention patterns.
